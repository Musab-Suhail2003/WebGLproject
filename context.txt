# Charizard's Chase — Project Context

## Overview
A WebGL mini-game built with Three.js. Charizard is about to eat a Magikarp when
Golbat swoops in and steals it. The player flies Charizard through an infinite
obstacle field to chase down Golbat and reclaim the meal. When caught, the outro
shows Charizard standing on top of the subdued Golbat, cooking the Magikarp with
his own flame.

---

## Narrative
- **Intro:** Charizard stands over a flopping Magikarp. Golbat dives in, snatches
  it, and escapes. Player must chase.
- **Gameplay:** Fly Charizard, dodge obstacles, collect coins for boost (Shift),
  close the gap to Golbat who dangles Magikarp beneath it.
- **Outro:** Charizard catches Golbat. Standing Charizard perches on Golbat's back,
  holds Magikarp over the tail flame, and cooks it. "Dinner is served! 🍽️"

---

## Tech Stack
- Three.js r0.160.0 (CDN importmap)
- GLTFLoader — all characters and props
- AnimationMixer — Charizard flying animation
- EffectComposer + UnrealBloomPass — post-processing
- Pure ES modules, no bundler

## Controls
| Key        | Action                        |
|------------|-------------------------------|
| A / D      | Yaw left / right              |
| W / S      | Pitch up / down               |
| Q / E      | Roll left / right             |
| ↑ / ↓      | Accelerate / decelerate       |
| Shift      | Boost (spend collected coins) |
| Escape     | Pause / resume                |

---

## Game Systems
- **Hearts:** 3 lives; hit loses one heart + 1.8 s invincibility (blink). 0 hearts → pause menu.
- **Boost:** Collect spinning gold coins → fills boost bar. Hold Shift to burn it for 32 m/s burst.
- **Golbat AI:** Velocity-based, target 20 m ahead + sinusoidal weave. Max 10 m/s normal,
  14 m/s panic (<10 m from player). Obstacle repulsion keeps it from clipping terrain.
- **Obstacles:** 80 pooled — trees, GLB rocks, mountains (cone taper collision). Infinite recycle.
- **Clouds:** 20 passthrough GLB clouds drifting slowly at 6–12 m altitude.
- **Pause / Retry:** Escape or game-over auto-opens pause menu. Retry skips intro.
