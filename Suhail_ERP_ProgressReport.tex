% ── 1. Summary of Progress ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Summary of Progress}

The game is fully playable end-to-end with a complete narrative arc. Charizard
is about to eat a Magikarp when Golbat swoops in and steals it; the player
chases Golbat through an infinite procedural world and, on catching it, watches
a closing cutscene where Charizard stands on top of Golbat and cooks the
Magikarp with his own flame. Core gameplay features include a velocity-based
Golbat AI with panic behaviour, a coin-collection boost system, a three-heart
health system with invincibility frames, and a pause/retry menu. The world uses
GLB rock and cloud assets, infinite ground scrolling, and mountains with tapered
cone collision. All three advanced techniques --- toon shading, bloom, and
shadow mapping --- are active and visible.

% ── 3. Advanced Techniques ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Advanced Techniques Status}

\textbf{Toon Shading (3/5) — Completed.}
\texttt{MeshToonMaterial} is applied to all procedural scene geometry. All loaded
GLB models (Charizard, Golbat, Magikarp, rocks, clouds) have their materials
post-processed on load to \texttt{roughness = 1}, \texttt{metalness = 0}, and
\texttt{envMapIntensity = 0}, enforcing a consistent flat, matte toon look across
both procedural and imported assets.
\textit{Challenge:} PBR materials from GLB exports clashed with the toon aesthetic.
Resolved with a post-load \texttt{traverse} that overwrites material parameters.

\medskip
\textbf{Bloom (4/5) — Completed.}
\texttt{EffectComposer} with \texttt{UnrealBloomPass} (strength 0.8, radius 0.4,
threshold 0.85). The tail flame mesh uses an emissive colour so it glows through
bloom. During the outro cooking scene the \texttt{PointLight} intensity is animated
from 3 to 11, creating a visible bloom pulse as the flame ``cooks'' the Magikarp.
\textit{Challenge:} Bloom overexposed toon shading at high strength. Balanced by
lowering strength and raising threshold.

\medskip
\textbf{Shadow Mapping (4/5) — Completed.}
\texttt{PCFSoftShadowMap} with a 1024\texttimes1024 depth map from the directional
sun light; frustum manually sized (\(\pm40\) units). All characters, Magikarp, and
obstacles cast shadows; the infinite ground plane receives them.
\textit{Challenge:} Shadow acne at shallow sun angles mitigated by
\texttt{PCFSoftShadowMap}'s built-in bias; manual \texttt{shadow.bias} tuning
remains for the final submission.

% ── 5. Challenges ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Challenges Encountered}

A forward-vector orientation bug caused Charizard to fly sideways until a canonical
\(+Z\) forward convention was established with quaternion-derived movement. Golbat's
AI initially maintained a fixed offset regardless of player speed, making it
uncatchable; this was replaced with a velocity-capped system so a boosted Charizard
can genuinely close the gap. Tagging bounding-box collision against tall mountains
caused phantom crashes far from the geometry; replaced with a per-type cylinder/tapered-cone check that matches each obstacle's visual shape. Attaching the tail
flame to the animated tail bone has proven difficult because the GLB skeleton lives
inside \texttt{SkinnedMesh.skeleton.bones} rather than the scene graph; a positional
offset remains as an interim solution.
